> record of agreed terms. Both parties are strongly advised to have this reviewed and formalized
> by a licensed attorney in their jurisdiction before executing commercial contracts, raising
> capital, or onboarding outside investors. This document does not replace formal articles of
> incorporation or a shareholders' agreement.

## 2. Ownership — Equal Partnership

The Partners agree to an equal, undivided ownership of FASS Tech:

| Partner | Ownership Share |
|---|---|
| Sebastien Pambu | 50% |
| Archy Ntabona | 50% |

This split applies to:
- All equity in FASS Tech
- All revenue generated by FASS Tech and its products (including SuiviPro)
- All intellectual property developed under FASS Tech
- All assets acquired by FASS Tech
- All liabilities incurred by FASS Tech

This 50/50 split is the foundational and irrevocable starting position of the partnership.
It may only be altered by mutual written agreement signed by both Partners (see Section 7).

---

## 4. Decision-Making

### Day-to-Day Decisions
Each Partner may make decisions within their functional area (see Section 3) up to
**$2,000 USD** in spend or commitment without requiring the other's approval.
Decisions above this threshold require mutual agreement.

### Major Decisions — Require Both Partners' Written Consent
The following always require explicit written agreement from both Partners:

- Equity issuance or dilution of any kind (new shares, options, warrants)
- Bringing in a new partner, co-founder, or investor
- Taking on debt or financial obligations above $5,000 USD
- Signing contracts with a value above $10,000 USD
- Hiring full-time employees (part-time and contractors below $2,000/month exempt)
- Acquiring or disposing of material assets
- Changing the company name, product name, or legal structure
- Dissolving or winding down the company
- Entering any exclusivity arrangement with a third party

### Deadlock Resolution
If the Partners cannot agree on a major decision after good-faith discussion of at least
7 days, they will appoint a mutually agreed neutral third party to advise. The third
party's recommendation is advisory, not binding. If deadlock continues for 30 days, the
matter may be escalated to mediation per Section 9.

---

## 5. Financial Arrangements

### Revenue Sharing
All net revenue generated by FASS Tech or SuiviPro is shared 50/50 after:
- Operating expenses (hosting, tools, contractors, legal, accounting)
- Any agreed reinvestment into product development or business growth

Neither Partner may draw salary, distributions, or expense reimbursements without mutual
agreement on the amounts and timing.

### Capital Contributions
Any capital contributed by either Partner to fund company operations shall be recorded
in writing at the time of contribution, specifying:
- Amount contributed
- Whether it is a loan (to be repaid) or a capital contribution (no repayment)
- Terms of repayment if applicable

Capital contributions do not alter the 50/50 ownership split unless explicitly agreed
in writing by both Partners.

### Banking & Financial Controls
- A joint business bank account shall be opened in the company name
- Transactions above $1,000 USD require acknowledgement (not necessarily approval) of both Partners
- Transactions above $5,000 USD require dual-authorization
- Monthly financial statements shall be shared between Partners within 10 days of month-end

---

## 6. Intellectual Property

All intellectual property created by either Partner in connection with FASS Tech or
SuiviPro — including code, designs, branding, documentation, methodologies, trade secrets,
and client data — is the exclusive property of FASS Tech.

This applies regardless of:
- Whether the IP was created during business hours or personal time, if it was created
  in furtherance of FASS Tech's business
- Which Partner created it
- Whether it was created before or after the signing of this agreement, provided it
  relates to the products and services described herein

Neither Partner may assign, license, sell, or transfer any FASS Tech intellectual property
to a third party without written consent of both Partners.

---

## 7. Bringing in Outside Partners or Investors

The Partners anticipate that future growth may involve outside investment, strategic
partnerships, or co-development agreements. The following governs how that works:

### New Equity Partners or Investors
- Any introduction of a new equity holder requires written consent of both Partners
- New equity is issued from a pool agreed by both Partners — neither Partner's stake
  may be diluted more than the other's without their individual written consent
- A formal shareholders' agreement must be executed before any new equity is issued

### Anti-Dilution Protection
If new equity is issued (e.g., to an investor or new partner), both Founders' shares
dilute equally and proportionally from their respective 50% positions, unless both
Partners agree in writing to a different arrangement.

**Example:** If FASS Tech raises a round that issues 20% equity to an investor,
both Partners dilute equally: Sebastien → 40%, Archy → 40%, Investor → 20%.
Neither Partner may be diluted more than the other without their explicit written consent.

### Strategic Partnerships (Non-Equity)
Commercial partnerships, reseller agreements, and integration partnerships that do not
involve equity may be approved per the decision-making rules in Section 4.

### Right of First Refusal
If either Partner wishes to sell or transfer any portion of their equity to a third party,
the other Partner has the right to purchase that equity first, at the same price and terms
offered by the third party. The selling Partner must provide 30 days written notice.

## 8. Departure, Exit & Dissolution

### Voluntary Departure
If either Partner wishes to exit the company:
- They must provide 90 days written notice
- During the notice period, they continue their responsibilities and receive any agreed
  compensation
- Their equity stake does not automatically transfer — the remaining Partner has the right
  to purchase the departing Partner's shares at fair market value, to be determined by a
  mutually agreed independent valuation
- If the remaining Partner cannot or will not purchase the shares within 60 days of
  valuation, the departing Partner may sell to a qualified third party, subject to the
  other Partner's approval (not to be unreasonably withheld)

### Involuntary Departure (Incapacity or Death)
- In the event of permanent incapacity or death of either Partner, their equity passes
  to their designated heir or estate
- The surviving Partner has the right of first refusal to purchase the inherited shares
  at fair market value within 90 days
- The company shall not be dissolved solely due to the death or incapacity of one Partner

### Company Sale or Acquisition
- Any sale of FASS Tech or SuiviPro to a third party requires written consent of both Partners
- Proceeds from any such sale are distributed 50/50 after settlement of all outstanding
  liabilities and agreed expenses
- Neither Partner may negotiate or accept acquisition offers on behalf of the company
  without informing and involving the other Partner from the outset

### Dissolution
- Voluntary dissolution requires written consent of both Partners
- Upon dissolution, all assets are liquidated, all liabilities settled, and remaining
  proceeds distributed 50/50
- All intellectual property that cannot be sold shall be assigned equally to both Partners
  for independent use, unless otherwise agreed

---

## 9. Dispute Resolution

The Partners commit to resolving all disputes in good faith through direct conversation first.

If a dispute cannot be resolved within 14 days of written notice from one Partner to the other:
1. **Mediation** — Both Partners appoint a mutually agreed mediator. Cost shared equally.
   Mediation is non-binding but findings are recorded in writing.
2. **Arbitration** — If mediation fails within 30 days, the dispute is submitted to binding
   arbitration under the rules of a mutually agreed arbitration body.
3. **Jurisdiction** — Disputes are governed by the laws of the State of Georgia, United States
   of America. The Partners agree to submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and
   federal courts located in Georgia.

---

## 10. Confidentiality

Both Partners agree to keep confidential all non-public information about FASS Tech,
SuiviPro, clients, financial data, and technical systems — both during the partnership
and for a period of 3 years following any departure. This obligation survives the
termination of this agreement.

---

## 11. Non-Compete

During the term of this partnership, neither Partner may:
- Found, join, or advise a competing company offering substantially similar products
  or services in the same target markets without written consent of the other Partner
- Solicit FASS Tech clients, employees, or contractors for a competing venture

For a period of 12 months following voluntary departure, the departing Partner agrees
not to actively solicit FASS Tech's existing clients. This does not prevent the departing
Partner from working in the same industry generally.
