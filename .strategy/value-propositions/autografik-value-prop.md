## Executive Summary

Autografik is a **professional email signature generator** targeting students, new graduates, and young professionals who need polished signatures without design skills. Users select an industry preset, customize, and copy to their email client—no account required.

**Key Value Drivers:**
- $0 infrastructure cost (Vercel/Cloudflare free tier)
- No subscription model ($29 lifetime vs $48-108/year competitors)
- Viral referral mechanics built-in
- Adjacent market expansion potential (Professional Identity Suite)

## Revenue Model

### Pricing Tiers

| Tier | Price | Features |
|------|-------|----------|
| **Free** | $0 | All features + "Made with Autografik" branding |
| **Pro** | $29 lifetime | Remove branding, premium fonts, photo/logo upload |

### Why Lifetime Pricing Works

1. **No subscription fatigue** — one-time purchase feels like a deal
2. **Lower than competitors' annual cost** — WiseStamp $48/yr, MySignature $48/yr
3. **Viral branding** — free users become marketing through "Made with Autografik"
4. **Low infrastructure cost** — $0 hosting means any sale is profit
