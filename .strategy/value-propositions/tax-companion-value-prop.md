## Executive Summary

Tax Companion is a **pre-filing tax organizer** that helps individuals organize their financial documents before meeting with a CPA or filing taxes. Unlike competitors (TurboTax, H&R Block), it does NOT file taxes—it operates in the **uncontested pre-filing layer**.

**Key Value Drivers:**
- Zero-knowledge encryption (data never leaves device)
- No regulatory burden (no EFIN, IRS certification)
- Architectural moat competitors cannot easily copy
- Perfect market timing (trust crisis in incumbents)

## Revenue Model

### Pricing Tiers

| Tier | Price | Features |
|------|-------|----------|
| **Free** | \$0 | 1 bank statement/year, basic features, no CPA export |
| **Pro** | \$49/year | Unlimited statements, AI categorization, CPA-ready export |

### Why This Pricing Works

1. **Annual billing** aligns with tax season usage
2. **\$49 < CPA time savings** (\$200-400 saved on prep)
3. **No monthly subscription** avoids off-season churn
4. **One-time AppSumo LTD** option (\$99-199) for launch

### Revenue Channels

| Channel | Model | Potential |

| Direct (Pro tier) | \$49/year | Primary revenue |
| CPA Partner Program | Revenue share + white-label | \$500-1,500/firm/year |
| AppSumo LTD | \$99-199 one-time | Launch vehicle |

1. CPA direct outreach (highest intent, lowest cost)
