## Recurring Events (Best Value)

### Atlanta Startup Village (#ATLSV)

- **Frequency:** Monthly (3rd Monday)
- **Location:** Atlanta Tech Village, Buckhead
- **Cost:** FREE
- **Format:** Happy hour → startup pitches → networking
- **Audience:** 100+ entrepreneurs, VCs, Techstars mentors, Google VPs

**Why attend:** One of the largest startup gatherings in the Southeast. Pitch both apps to investors and potential users. Same venue where Calendly, SalesLoft, PrizePicks, and Terminus were built.

**Action items:**
- [ ] RSVP on Meetup: [atlanta-startup-village](https://www.meetup.com/atlanta-startup-village/)
- [ ] Sign up to pitch at check-in (3-minute pitch + breakout room)

---

### Atlanta Tech Meetup

- **Frequency:** 1st Tuesday of every month, 6 PM
- **Location:** Atlanta Tech Park, 107 Technology Pkwy, Peachtree Corners
- **Cost:** FREE (pizza and beverages provided)
- **Format:** Guest speaker + networking

**Why attend:** Monthly touchpoint with Atlanta tech community. Low-effort networking—show up, demo apps on your phone, collect contacts.

**Action items:**
- [ ] RSVP via their new platform (not Meetup directly)
- [ ] Bring business cards with QR codes to app landing pages

---

### Pitch Practice with Jacey Cadet

- **Frequency:** Weekly (Tuesdays)
- **Location:** Atlanta Tech Village
- **Cost:** FREE
- **Format:** Practice 30-second to 2-minute pitches, get feedback

**Why attend:** Refine your Tax Companion and Autografik elevator pitches before bigger events. No commitment—drop in anytime.

---

### Find a Co-Founder Meetup (Startup Oasis)

- **Frequency:** 3rd Thursday monthly, 5 PM
- **Location:** ATDC, Tech Square (Georgia Tech)
- **Cost:** FREE
- **Format:** Shark Tank-style pitches → breakout rooms

**Why attend:** Find potential co-founders, advisors, or early adopters. Good for recruiting technical talent if needed.

**Action items:**
- [ ] RSVP on Meetup: [atlanta-startup-cofounders](https://www.meetup.com/atlanta-startup-cofounders/)

## Preparation Checklist

### Before Any Event

- [ ] Business cards with QR codes (separate for each app)
- [ ] One-page product sheets (PDF on phone)
- [ ] 30-second pitch memorized
- [ ] 2-minute pitch ready
- [ ] Demo devices charged
- [ ] Landing page URLs ready to share

### Follow-Up (Within 48 Hours)

- [ ] Connect on LinkedIn with all contacts
- [ ] Send personalized email with app link
- [ ] Add to CRM/spreadsheet
- [ ] Schedule follow-up if interested
